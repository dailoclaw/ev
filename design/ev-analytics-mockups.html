The Statement

ledger-first

Reads like an annual bank statement. A period toggle picks the year; a big total sits up top, then every month is an itemised ledger row — cost, kWh, and a mini spend sparkline — footed by a yearly total. For someone who thinks in rows and running totals. Tap any month to drill in.

Instrument Cluster

glanceable KPIs

A gauge cluster of stat tiles — total cost, kWh, avg rate, sessions, each with its own micro-sparkline and a delta vs the prior period. One tap on the metric toggle re-skins the big chart below between cost and kWh. Everything answerable at a glance, no scrolling to read the numbers.

Trend Explorer

chart-first

One big line chart owns the screen. A scrubber flag calls out the selected point; the metric switch flips between Cost / kWh / $-per-kWh, and provider chips recolour what's plotted. Below, plain-English delta cards ("June cost the most"). For exploring the shape of the trend, not reading a table.

Where It Goes

composition

Answers "where does it all go?" — a donut splits cost by charger network, race bars rank them, and a stacked column per month shows the free-vs-paid split of every kWh. Filter by month/year up top. The natural home for the free-energy story that defines this app.

Head-to-Head

comparison

Pick two periods and put them in the ring — this year vs last, or any two months. Each metric (cost, kWh, rate, charges, free saved) shows as a paired split bar with the winner called out. Built for the question "am I doing better or worse than before?"

Minimal Stats proposal

One story first. Detail on tap.

Stats should stop behaving like a dense analytics console in Minimal mode. The first screen should tell you whether charging is getting better over time, then let you drill into energy, free charging, providers, and records.

Hero answerLead with all-in rate: the clearest signal of charging economics.
One visualUse one large trend object instead of five view modes fighting for attention.
Rows as portalsEnergy, free charging, providers, and records become calm drill-ins.

How It Fits Minimal

The current Analytics modes do not disappear. They become second-level screens behind the calm overview rows, which keeps Minimal quiet without deleting power-user paths.

ClusterReplaced on first load by the one-number all-in-rate hero.
TrendsBecomes the large primary rate chart, with axes hidden until interaction.
SplitMoves behind Free energy, where free vs paid makes contextual sense.
CompareMoves behind Energy added or the trend chart tap target.
RecordsLives as a quiet row or footer action instead of first-screen trophy density.

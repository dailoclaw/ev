How It Fits Minimal

The Classic Vehicle page can keep the fuller calculator style. Minimal Vehicle turns the same calculations into a focused car cockpit with progressive disclosure.

Cost comparisonBecomes the primary “why this matters” visual: two calm cost bars.
AssumptionsStay editable, but use stepper rows instead of a dense card.
